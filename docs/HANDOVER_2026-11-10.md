Prepared **10 November 2026** for the team demo and document handover on **11 November 2026, 08:30 IST**. It records what is verified, what is partial, and what remains. Source of truth for ongoing work: [`docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`](PROJECT_CONTEXT.md) and [`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md).

## 1. What Artha AI is

A private personal-finance web app for first-time earners and beginners in their twenties (individual or family planning, INR). It helps answer four questions:

1. Where is my money going?
2. Can I afford to live in another city?
3. If rent is too high, what could fit my budget?
4. How do I start saving and investing, and plan for goals?

Principles carried through every feature: ask instead of guessing, never treat a blank as zero, user review before anything becomes a confirmed record, original statements deleted after parsing, and no unexplained scores or promised returns.

Stack: Flask modular monolith, SQLAlchemy, Alembic (schema **0013**), server-rendered Jinja, SQLite for the laptop demo, money stored as integer paise. Branch `integration/biswa-2026-11-09`.

## 2. Running the demo

1. **Close every old demo server first.** On 10 November three separate servers from 6 and 9 November were still serving port 5148, so a page could come from old code. Close their terminal windows or end the `python.exe` processes running `flask ... --port 5148` in Task Manager.
2. Double-click **`Open Artha Demo.cmd`** in the workspace. It starts the app at `http://localhost:5148` and opens Edge with the rehearsal profile.
3. Sign in to the rehearsal account (a restart always requires a fresh sign-in).
4. Open **Asha's household (fictional demo)**. Everything in it is invented: a November 2026 statement (approved, with one matched refund and one unresolved payment), October and November plans with allocations, four goals (one private), nine profile entries and one holding.

Recreate the demo household on another database with `scripts/seed_demo_household.py --owner-email <existing account>` (it refuses to run twice). Database backups made today are in `instance/` (`before-goals-0011-…`, `before-profile-0013-…`, `before-demo-seed-…`). Never include `instance/` in a shared package.

## 4. Feature status

**Working and tested** (full suite 210 passed, 1 PostgreSQL-only skip, 10 November):

- Local email/password accounts, households, owner/viewer roles, CSRF protection, login throttling.
- CSV and Excel import in defined layouts; validation, duplicate detection, review, edit, defer, approve; originals never stored.
- Local category classifier (Linear SVM, 95.24% on the held-out synthetic test set) suggesting categories for review.
- Ledger with type separate from category, refund matching capped at the purchase amount, user-confirmed transfer pairs, money-flow view.
- Monthly plan (individual/family, grocery detail, housing rules without double counting) with carry-forward from the previous month; savings allocation.
- Monthly summary with categories, unresolved entries, exclusions and partial-coverage labels.
- City rent comparison (221 cities, rent only), saved relocation scenarios, rental search links.
- Saved goals: purposes, deadline-first plan, ongoing costs, borrowing comparison, shared or private.
- Financial profile: income, assets, loans, periodic bills, RDs and reserve, with explicit answer states and private entries.
- Manual portfolio; reserve/RD/goal calculators; recurring-payment review; learning pages.
- Phone-width layouts checked in browser emulation (360/390/1280px).

**Partial:**

- Monthly plan still saves blank answers as ₹0.
- Financial profile does not yet feed the monthly plan, goals or reserve calculator; no net-worth view.
- Goals: no delete button, no progress from real contributions, no priority-based allocation.
- City comparison covers rent only; the dataset snapshot date is unknown.

**Not done:** physical Android testing; live email/SMS; bank PDF and handwritten/photo records; any LLM agent; live bank, broker or market data.

## 7. Roadmap after the demo

- **Typing in manual records** (notebook/paper) through the same review-and-approve flow (about 3–4 h).
- **Reading photos, scans and PDFs** (OCR): needs a decision on a local engine versus an outside service, and handwriting accuracy is uncertain.
- **Specific bank PDF statement formats**, once the required banks are named.
- **Live email verification and phone/SMS login.**
- **Retirement planning, risk questions and assets/liabilities in full**, with an approved, sourced methodology.
- **Conversational help or LLM agents** (relocation, portfolio, monthly analyst): not approved; needs provider, privacy and evaluation decisions. Financial data must not be sent to a model by default.
- **Full city living-cost estimates** with validated data; privacy-preserving use of other users' data remains research only.
- **Installable app (PWA) or native Android/iOS.**
