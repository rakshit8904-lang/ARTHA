## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intelligent, reassuring, and precision-driven fintech environment tailored for India’s emerging generation of wealth-builders: students, new earners, and ambitious young professionals. The aesthetic balances institutional banking credibility with the fluid minimalism of next-generation intelligent financial software.

### Tone & Personality
- **Empowering & Lucid:** Complex personal finance, tax regimes, SIPs, and credit metrics are distilled into transparent, glanceable surfaces without financial jargon.
- **Prudent & Forward-Looking:** Visual poise that communicates security, wealth accumulation, and computational intelligence rather than speculative volatility.
- **Tactile Modern Banking:** Crisp white surfaces, whisper-quiet borders, disciplined typographic contrast, and restrained chromatic elevation.

### Design Movement
**Vibrant Fintech Minimalist:** High-contrast typographic scales, pristine whitespace ratios, surgical slate dividers, and purposeful vibrant emerald accents that signal upward growth and liquidity.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around capital clarity, positive compounding, and effortless risk stratification. The default mode is light to evoke ledger openness, clarity under sunlight, and clean mathematical order.

### Core Functional Palette
- **Primary Vibrant Emerald (`#04e3a4`):** Represents wealth growth, portfolio appreciation, healthy cash flow, and successful transactions with an energetic, modern pop.
- **Secondary / Predictive Tones:** Reserved for predictive AI signals, automated workflows, and scheduled mandates.
- **Tertiary Horizon Blue (`#e1f5f9`):** Applied to investment classifications, SIP tracks, and verifiable KYC indicators.
- **Neutral Charcoal & Slate (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`, `#64748B`, `#F8FAFC`):** Deep charcoal ensures absolute legibility for financial figures, while soft slate canvas tints eliminate retina glare during prolonged analysis.

### Semantic Tones & Financial Intent
- **Success / Positive (`#04e3a4`):** Net worth gains, completed payouts, credit score leaps.
- **Informative (`#e1f5f9`):** Bank sync status, upcoming EMI schedules, recurring reminders.
- **Attention / Caution (`#F59E0B`):** Impending tax deductions, credit card utilization thresholds (>30%), subscription renewals.
- **Critical / Negative (`#EF4444`):** Overdraft alerts, high-interest debt warnings, payment failures.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear distinction between narrative structural hierarchy and precision mathematical data.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering friendly yet professional geometric balance that welcomes early investors while preserving institutional authority.
- **Narratives & Interface Copy:** Set in **Inter** for supreme micro-legibility at 12px to 16px across mobile viewports and denser dashboard tables.
- **Currencies & Telemetry:** Financial counters, ledger entries, tabular data, and ticker outputs utilize **JetBrains Mono** to guarantee vertical alignment of decimal points across rapid financial balance shifts.

### Currency Formatting Rules
- Always prepend the Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`) directly without extra spacing: `₹45,250.00`.
- Use the Indian numbering system standard (Lakhs and Crores) for local portfolio visualizations (`₹1,50,000` rather than `₹150,000`; `₹2.40 Cr` for aggregate assets).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an 8pt modular grid that enforces consistency across dynamic dashboard widgets and transactional ledgers.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive layout with `2rem` outer page margins, `1.5rem` gutters, and a maximum central content constraint of `1280px`. Left rail navigation docks permanently at `280px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Navigation collapses to a slim side icon strip or persistent contextual top bar.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` gutters. Primary views emphasize single-column stacked financial metrics and sticky bottom navigation sheets.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Dense, data-heavy widgets (stock tracking, transaction feeds) employ `space-xs` and `space-sm` for element coupling.
- High-level metric cards and portfolio hero units maintain generous `space-lg` and `space-xl` breathing margins to lower financial anxiety.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages restrained surface layering and low-contrast borders instead of heavy, dramatic drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (soft slate) serves as the primary ground for all pages.
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards, Modules):** `#FFFFFF` bordered with an imperceptible `1px solid #E2E8F0` stroke.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Modals, Flyouts):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Critical Alert Toasts & Keypad Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` accompanied by `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1)`.

### Ghost Elevation & Dividers
Avoid heavy dividers. Line separators must always be set to `#F1F5F9`. Card elevation is achieved by contrast against the `#F8FAFC` canvas rather than vertical displacement.

## Shapes

The design uses a refined curvature profile (`roundedness: 2`) that pairs modern digital fintech accessibility with structural integrity.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Action Buttons):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Surface Modules & Cards:** `rounded-lg` at `1rem` (16px) creates a balanced, contained enclosure for multi-datapoint card architectures.
- **Hero Containers & Slide Sheets:** `rounded-xl` at `1.5rem` (24px) communicates seamless mobile responsiveness.
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** Explicitly fully rounded (`9999px`) to distinguish interactive badges and category chips from structural cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Financial Action:** Background `#04e3a4`, foreground `#0f172a`, font-weight 600. On hover: a deeper energetic emerald tone with a subtle transform `translateY(-1px)`.
- **Secondary Action:** Background `#F1F5F9`, foreground `#0F172A`. On hover: `#E2E8F0`.
- **Destructive Action:** Subtle `#FEE2E2` tint with `#DC2626` text; avoids unnecessary visual panic until critical confirmation states.
- **Height & Sizing:** Standard input/button height is 44px on desktop and 48px on mobile touch targets.

### Metric Badges & Status Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 10px`, font size `11px`, font-weight 600, uppercase tracking `0.04em`.
- **Positive Yield:** Background `#E6FFF5`, text `#046848`, prepended by standard vector upward delta arrow `↑`.
- **Negative Incline:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#B91C1C`, prepended by downward vector arrow `↓`.
- **Neutral Allocation:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`.

### Input Fields & Monetary Forms
- **Resting:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`.
- **Active / Focused:** Border `1.5px solid #04e3a4`, ring glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(4, 227, 164, 0.15)`.
- **Prefix Lock:** Indian Rupee symbol `₹` rests pinned to the left edge in fixed `#64748B`, with primary input value set in monospace tabular alignment.

### Cards & Wealth Containers
- Bordered with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, interior padding `20px` to `24px`.
- Card headers strictly decouple categorization labels from numerical values using `12px` font metadata above prominent `24px` Plus Jakarta Sans balances.

### Progress Bars & Budget Gauges
- Height: `8px`, track background `#E2E8F0`, corner radius `9999px`.
- **Fill Bar:** Dynamic gradient matching vibrant primary accents. Switches automatically to `#F59E0B` at 75% budget exhaustion, and `#EF4444` when exceeding 90% allocation limit.
